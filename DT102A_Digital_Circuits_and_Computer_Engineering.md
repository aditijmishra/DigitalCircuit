## General Information
The course is given on campus and forms part of the Bachelor Programme in Computer Science and Engineering, specialising in the Internet of Things, Year 1.

## Level of Study
**G1N First cycle**, has only upper-secondary level entry requirements.

## Entry Requirements
General entry requirements and Physics 2, Chemistry 1 or Natural Sciences 2, Mathematics 3c or Mathematics D.  
*Or:* Physics level 2, Chemistry level 1 or Natural Sciences level 2, Mathematics Further level 1c.

---

## Aim
The aim of the course is for students to develop a basic understanding of computer engineering and digital engineering and to develop a basic knowledge of working methods in engineering.

---

## Content
The course covers:
- Number systems
- Data storage
- Truth tables
- Boolean algebra
- Combinatorial networks
- Sequential networks
- Principles for processors central processing units
- Memory and I/O

---

## Intended Learning Outcomes

### Knowledge and Understanding
After completing the course, students will be able to:
1. Explain different types of logic gates and their applications in digital technology (1).
2. Explain state machines, latches, and flip-flops and their applications in digital technology (2).
3. Describe the main components of a computer and their functions, and how programs are executed in a computer (3).
4. Explain how different data types are stored (4).

### Skills and Abilities
After completing the course, students will be able to:
5. Perform basic calculations and conversions in different number systems (5).
6. Perform simple calculations and simplifications using Boolean algebra (6).
7. Implement Boolean functions using logic gates (7).
8. Perform digital designs using state machines and logic circuits (8).

## Implementation
Teaching is in the form of lectures, laboratory work, and exercises.

## Examination – Tests and Forms
The grades used for the course as a whole are **Fail (U)**, **3**, **4**, and **5**.

### Modules
1. **Examination 1 (4.5 credits):** *Introduction to digital and computer engineering*
   - On-campus written examination.
   - Learning outcomes 1–6 are examined.
   - Grades U, 3, 4, and 5 are awarded.

2. **Examination 2 (3.0 credits):** *Practical digital engineering*
   - Individual laboratory work with individual written and oral presentations.
   - Learning outcomes 5–8 are examined.
   - The grades Fail (U) and Pass (G) are awarded.

### Grading Criteria for Overall Course
- **Grade 3:** Requires Grade 3 on Examination 1 and Pass (G) on Examination 2.
- **Grade 4:** Requires Grade 4 on Examination 1 and Pass (G) on Examination 2.
- **Grade 5:** Requires Grade 5 on Examination 1 and Pass (G) on Examination 2.

*The university's exam regulations are available at [www.hkr.se/student](https://www.hkr.se/student).*

## Transitional Rules
Students accepted according to this syllabus have the right to be examined according to this syllabus for one (1) year after the syllabus has ceased to be valid either because it has been replaced by a new one or the course has been discontinued. 

Students who have not completed the course with a satisfactory result within a year after the syllabus has ceased to apply can be given a new opportunity for examination, within two further years, with the approval of the examiner, where the examination will be based on course-content judged to be close, and as long as no more than a marginal increase of resources used will occur. After these further two years, students can only get a grade for successfully completed partial examinations.
